Notes

Sails: ORC coefficients (lift and drag versus apparent wind angle, profile and induced drag with the effective rig height). Air: mast (Cd 0.8 bare, 0.4 behind the mainsail), spreaders, shrouds and stays (Cd 1.2), boom, bowsprit, topsides (Cd 0.8) and skipper. CAFAR: strip-theory T-foil (each strip with its own depth, dihedral and flap), strut and elevators. Water: hull from hydrostatic tables and CFD or ORC resistance, keel, bulb, daggerboard and rudders with lifting line and ITTC friction. Starting dimensions: typical Mini 6.50 prototype; replace them with the real boat data.
